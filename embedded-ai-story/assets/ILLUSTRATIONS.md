## Generation prompt

Create ONE clean illustration contact sheet for a university engineering lecture in French about embedded AI. EXACTLY 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal rectangular panels, white background, NO borders and no panels touching. Every illustration centered within its own cell with generous white margin. Absolutely NO text, NO lettering, NO labels, NO watermarks. Style: warm editorial vector-like illustration, organic human poses, subtle hand-drawn contours, lively but tasteful coral orange, turquoise, mustard yellow and deep navy on clean pure white, flat colours with gentle shading, professional educational textbook rather than a tech startup dashboard. Consistent fictional young woman engineer Alice throughout: warm brown skin, curly dark shoulder-length hair, teal shirt, orange trousers, white trainers, natural believable anatomy. Six distinct vignettes in reading order: (1) Alice at home carrying a large cardboard box with both hands, turning toward a floor lamp she wants to switch on, small house plant nearby; (2) Alice with two friends of different appearance speaking toward a small tabletop smart device, small speech bubbles with only abstract sound waves, no text; (3) Alice seated at a simple desk opening the small smart electronic device beside a laptop, curious and concentrating; (4) Alice looks surprised at a small unresponsive tabletop smart device while the home wifi router has a small red disconnection icon, floor lamp dark; (5) Alice at an engineer workbench holding a tiny green circuit board, laptop and an open small enclosure alongside, pleased to have made it fit; (6) Alice smiling and explaining a simple curve on a whiteboard to two attentive friends, small circuit board on table. Each illustration should read instantly as one everyday situation. Keep faces appealing and expressive, eyes simple and natural, hands correct, no anthropomorphic robots or glowing brains, no fantasy technology. A polished cheerful human educational illustration set, matching characters and visual language across all six panels. Render at landscape aspect ratio.
